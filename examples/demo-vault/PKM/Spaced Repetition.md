# Spaced Repetition

Memories decay along a **forgetting curve** (Ebbinghaus); every successful
review flattens the curve, so reviews can be spaced further and further apart.

- **Active recall**, trying to retrieve the answer before seeing it, is far more
  effective than re-reading.
- Anki schedules each card individually based on how hard it was last time.
- Good cards are small and precise. One fact per card, just like one idea per
  note.

Ten minutes a day beats an hour on Sunday.
