# Indian Independence Movement

The struggle to end British rule in India, from the founding of the Indian National Congress (1885) to independence in 1947.

Phases:
- Early moderates petitioned for reforms within the empire; the 1905 partition of Bengal radicalised the movement (swadeshi boycotts).
- **Gandhi** returned from South Africa in 1915 and turned it into a mass movement built on *satyagraha*, non-violent resistance: the Non-Cooperation Movement (1920-22), the **Salt March** (1930) and Quit India (1942).
- Others took different paths: Bhagat Singh's revolutionaries, Subhas Chandra Bose's Indian National Army allied with Japan.

After 1945 Britain, exhausted by the war, negotiated withdrawal. The movement's failure to keep Hindu and Muslim politics united led to the Partition of India alongside independence in August 1947. Gandhi was assassinated in January 1948 by a Hindu nationalist.
