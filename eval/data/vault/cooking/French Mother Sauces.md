# French Mother Sauces

Escoffier codified five mother sauces that most classic sauces derive from:

1. **Bechamel**: milk thickened with a white roux. Leads to Mornay (add cheese).
2. **Veloute**: white stock (chicken, fish, veal) thickened with a blond roux.
3. **Espagnole**: brown stock with a brown roux, tomato and mirepoix; reduced with more stock it becomes demi-glace.
4. **Sauce tomate**: tomatoes, stock, aromatics.
5. **Hollandaise**: egg yolks emulsified with warm clarified butter and lemon. Leads to bearnaise.

The first four are thickened by starch (roux); hollandaise is entirely different, an emulsion held together by the lecithin in the yolks, which is why it can break if it gets too hot or the butter goes in too fast.

Knowing the families means a recipe is just mother sauce + a few additions.
