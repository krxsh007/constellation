# Kimchi Fermentation

Kimchi is lacto-fermented: salt pulls water out of the napa cabbage and suppresses spoilage organisms, while naturally present lactic acid bacteria (Leuconostoc early, then Lactobacillus) convert sugars into lactic acid. The falling pH preserves the cabbage and gives the tang. Same microbiology as a sourdough starter, just salt instead of flour.

Process I use:
1. Quarter cabbage, salt between leaves (~2-3% of weight), rest 6-8 h, rinse.
2. Paste: gochugaru, garlic, ginger, fish sauce, a little sugar or grated pear, glutinous rice porridge.
3. Pack tightly into a jar, press out air, leave 1-2 days at room temperature, then fridge.

It keeps getting more sour for weeks. Over-fermented kimchi is perfect for kimchi jjigae or kimchi fried rice. Bubbling and a fizzy smell are normal; fuzzy mould on top is not.
