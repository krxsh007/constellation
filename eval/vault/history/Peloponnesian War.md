# Peloponnesian War

The war (431-404 BC) between Athens and its maritime empire (the Delian League) and Sparta with its Peloponnesian League. Thucydides, an Athenian general turned historian, wrote the account: the "truest cause" was Spartan fear of growing Athenian power.

Phases:
1. **Archidamian War** (431-421): Sparta ravaged Attica each summer; Pericles kept Athenians behind the Long Walls and used the navy. A plague killed perhaps a quarter of the city, including Pericles. Ended by the Peace of Nicias.
2. **Sicilian Expedition** (415-413): the Assembly, swayed by Alcibiades, sent a huge force to Syracuse; it was annihilated.
3. **Ionian War** (413-404): Sparta, funded by Persian gold, built a fleet; Lysander destroyed the Athenian fleet at Aegospotami and starved Athens into surrender.

Athens lost its empire and briefly its democracy (the Thirty Tyrants). Thucydides' Melian Dialogue is still read as the classic statement of political realism.
