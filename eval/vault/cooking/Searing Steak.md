# Searing Steak

Goal: a deep brown crust with an evenly cooked interior.

1. **Dry brine**: salt the steak 1-24 h ahead, uncovered in the fridge. The surface dries, which is essential for browning.
2. **Pan**: a well-seasoned cast iron pan preheated until it just smokes. Thick iron stores heat so the temperature does not crash when the meat goes in.
3. **Oil**: thin layer of a high smoke point oil.
4. **Flip often**: flipping every 30-60 s actually cooks more evenly and still builds crust.
5. Butter, thyme and garlic in the last minute; baste.
6. Rest 5 minutes.

For thick steaks I reverse sear: low oven to 5-10 C below target, then a fast sear.

The crust is the Maillard reaction; a wet steak or a crowded pan steams at 100 C and never browns. Pull at 52 C for medium-rare; carryover adds a few degrees.
