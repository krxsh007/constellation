# Hash Browns

Proper diner hash browns: shredded potato, crisp and golden outside, tender inside. The word "hash" comes from the French hacher, to chop.

Method:
1. Grate starchy potatoes (russets) on the large holes.
2. Rinse in cold water to remove surface starch, then squeeze as dry as possible in a towel. Moisture is the enemy of crispness: the potato has to dry before it can brown, which is the Maillard reaction doing its thing.
3. Hot pan with plenty of fat (clarified butter or oil). A cast iron pan works best.
4. Spread in a thin layer, press down, and **don't touch it** for 5-6 minutes.
5. Flip in sections, cook the other side, salt at the end.

Shortcut: microwave or par-boil the potatoes first, cool, then grate; they hold together better. Leftover hash browns re-crisp well in the oven but never in the microwave.
