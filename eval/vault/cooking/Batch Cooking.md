# Batch Cooking

Batch cooking: cook a big batch of a few components on Sunday and assemble meals all week. Saves time and money and stops the 9 pm takeaway habit.

What batches well:
- A big pot of grains (rice, farro), kept 4 days in the fridge.
- Roast trays of vegetables.
- One braise or curry; these taste better on day two anyway.
- A batch of stock, and a couple of sauces frozen in ice cube trays so I can thaw just a portion.

What doesn't: anything crispy, delicate greens, and dressed salads.

My batch schedule:
1. Oven on first, vegetables in.
2. Grains on the stove.
3. Protein while those cook.
4. Cool fast, label with dates, fridge or freezer.

Batch size rule: cook for 4 days max in the fridge; freeze the rest. Food safety: cool within 2 hours.
