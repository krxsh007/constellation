# Stovetop Popcorn

Each popcorn kernel has a hard, moisture-proof hull around starchy endosperm with ~14% water. Heated to about 180 C, the water turns to steam, the pressure builds until the hull ruptures, and the starch puffs out and sets into foam.

My method:
1. Heavy pot, 3 tbsp coconut oil, medium-high heat, with 3 test kernels.
2. When the test kernels pop, add 1/2 cup kernels in one layer, cover, take off the heat for 30 seconds so they all come up to temperature together.
3. Back on the heat, shake often, lid slightly ajar so steam escapes (soggy popcorn otherwise).
4. When pops slow to 2-3 seconds apart, dump into a bowl.

Old kernels that have dried out pop poorly; store them airtight. Season while hot: fine salt sticks better than flaky, and nutritional yeast is the best topping.
